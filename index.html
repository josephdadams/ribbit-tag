      How to move

      • L REach thumb is a sticky frog hand. Left side of the screen is your left hand, right side is your right.
      • ↓Swipe down to shove the ground away and launch forward. Faster swipes make bigger leaps.
      • ↔Swipe sideways to turn around.
      • ↑Next to a tree or rock wall? Keep swiping down to climb it. The treehouse has steps.
      • !Your hands go see-through when there is nothing close enough to grab. The arrow at the top points to the lava frog.
      • HiTap a frog or press Talk to chat. Tell them to follow you, ask for a joke, or tease them.
      • PadController: left stick to push (up forward, down back, sideways), right stick to turn, A jump, hold RT for big pushes, X talk (D-pad for quick replies), Y player list, Start menu. On menus, the D-pad moves, A picks, B goes back.
      • KeyKeyboard: W push forward, S back, A D turn, Q E sideways, Space jump, hold Shift for big pushes. T talk, 1–9 quick replies, Tab next frog, H be host, M music on/off, P player list, I mode info, Esc menu. On the menu: 1 Infection, 2 Classic, 3 Capture the Lily Pad, 4 Freeze Tag, 5 Hot Potato, 6 Fly Feast, 7 Free Play, 8 King of the Lily Pad, Q Daily quests, O Play online, R Wardrobe.
